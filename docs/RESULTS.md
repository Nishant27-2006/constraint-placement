## 0. Benchmark geometry

The three affine suites differ in **codimension** -- the fraction of each hour's state that the physics fixes analytically. This turns out to be the variable that decides whether hard constraints help or hurt.

| suite | days | $D$/hour | constraints/hour | dof/hour | codim $r/D$ | channel scale ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `grid_noflow` | 1998 | 118 | 11 | 107 | 0.093 | 36.0x |
| `measured` | 1990 | 69 | 21 | 48 | 0.304 | 12.4x |
| `grid` | 1998 | 304 | 197 | 107 | 0.648 | 21.3x |
| `ac` | 1979 | 228 | 114 | 114 | 0.500 | 63.0x |

`grid_noflow` and `grid` are the *same* 118-bus system and the same measured injections; `grid` additionally carries the 186 line flows, which the 186 flow-definition rows of $A$ determine exactly. So the codimension contrast is obtained without changing the data-generating process -- only which variables the model is asked to emit.

Train-time projection (ours) against unconstrained flow matching:

* `grid_noflow` (codim 0.093): **-0.07%** ES, $t=-0.26$
* `measured` (codim 0.304): **+3.71%** ES, $t=+5.50$
* `grid` (codim 0.648): **-1.96%** ES, $t=-6.29$

The effect is not a constant that a better implementation would move; it **changes sign**, significantly in both directions, on the same code.

**The controlled contrast is `grid_noflow` vs `grid`.** Same 118-bus system, same measured injections, same backbone and budget; the only change is whether the model must also emit the 186 line flows that $A$ determines exactly. Codimension goes 0.093 -> 0.648 and train-time projection goes from indistinguishable to significantly better. That comparison is clean.

**`measured` is a genuinely different data-generating process** -- EIA-930 accounting identities across 6 balancing authorities, not Kirchhoff on a synthetic network -- so it is a confounded third point, not a third rung of one ladder. What it establishes is weaker but still important: there exist real problems on which exact projection significantly *costs* fidelity. We do not claim codimension alone predicts the magnitude.

**One alternative explanation checked and rejected.** Channel scale heterogeneity does not account for the pattern: `grid_noflow` has the widest scale spread (36x) and shows no effect, while `grid` (21x) shows the largest improvement.

## 8. What the paper should claim

1. **The question is well posed and the answer is not universal.** Where a hard physical constraint belongs is not a fixed methodological fact. On the controlled contrast, raising codimension turns train-time projection from irrelevant into a significant win; on a different real dataset it is a significant loss. Anyone reporting a single winner has measured one suite.
2. **Penalties are dominated on both axes** and should stop being the default.
3. **Feasibility is not decision value.** The correlation between scenario feasibility and unit-commitment cost is absent; calibration carries it. This is the finding the constrained-generation literature most needs.
4. **Exactness is free in NFE** for affine invariants, unlike inference-time correction.
5. Two pre-registered predictions (C3 ordering, C5' transfer) were **falsified by our own data and are reported as falsified**.
